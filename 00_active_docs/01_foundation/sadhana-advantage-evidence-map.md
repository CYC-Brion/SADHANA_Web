## Homepage Proof Rule

Every advantage must appear with all three elements:

1. A short buyer benefit, not a generic factory claim.
2. A relevant real image or product detail.
3. A route to the next buyer action.

Avoid using unsupported abstractions such as "best quality" or "one-stop supplier" without showing the mechanism or evidence behind them.
